> It is the authoritative reference for intake, quotation, booking, operations, document exchange, and settlement workflows.

## Purpose

Standardise how FDS receives, processes, and responds to KBI requests in freight forwarding. The SOP covers:

- Quotation requests
- Service confirmation
- Handover to Ops
- Document exchange
- Schedule updates
- Debt/invoice handling

## Operating Principles

1. **Single point of contact** – Each KBI order is assigned one dedicated FDS staff member to ensure timely, effective, and consistent communication.
2. **Formal email channel** – All notifications (quotation, shipment changes, confirmations) are exchanged via email between FDS and KBI for traceability.
3. **All-in pricing** – FDS quotations are all-inclusive and fixed, except when costs change due to:
   - KBI changing, adjusting, or omitting information in the quotation request
   - KBI delaying information provision per SOP
   - Errors by a party other than FDS

## Roles & Responsibilities

| Role / Department | Primary Responsibility | Timing | Output |
|---|---|---|---|
| **KBI** | Send quotation requests, supplement information, confirm quotation, review Draft BL/AWB/documents, respond to debt when needed. | From start to file closure. | Customer requests and confirmations. |
| **FDS Sales** | Receive request, assign Sales-in-charge, verify information, prepare/send quotation, confirm service, handover to Ops. | From request receipt to Ops handover; return for commercial issues or major changes affecting customer. | Quotation sent, service confirmed, complete handover. |
| **FDS Ops** | Receive handover, update schedule, process documents, send Draft/Final BL/AWB, track operational updates with KBI. | After KBI confirms service and Sales hands over. | Documents and operational updates completed. |
| **FDS Kế toán (Accounting)** | Handle Debit Notes, invoices, and debt reconciliation with KBI via accounting email flow. | When debt/invoice arises. | Debit Note/invoice responded to and filed. |

## Standard Workflow

```
KBI Request → FDS Sales intake → Quotation → KBI confirmation
    → Sales handover to Ops → Ops execution & document exchange
    → Delivery & settlement → File closure
```

### Phase 1 – Quotation (BÁO GIÁ)

1. KBI sends a quotation request (item, quantity, origin, destination, Incoterm, expected shipment date).
2. FDS Sales assigns a dedicated Sales-in-charge.
3. Sales verifies information, requests clarification if needed.
4. Sales prepares and sends quotation within SLA.
5. KBI reviews and confirms or requests changes.
6. Once confirmed, Sales prepares service confirmation and handover to Ops.

### Phase 2 – Transport & Operations (VẬN CHUYỂN)

1. Ops receives handover from Sales.
2. Ops books carrier/forwarder, confirms schedule.
3. Ops updates KBI on booking confirmation, ETD, ETA.
4. Ops handles document preparation (Draft BL/AWB, CI, PL).
5. Ops sends Draft BL/AWB to KBI for review within SLA.
6. KBI reviews and confirms or requests amendments.
7. Ops issues Final BL/AWB after KBI confirmation.
8. Ops monitors shipment, updates KBI on milestones and any changes.
9. Ops handles customs clearance coordination.
10. Ops arranges delivery (EDO/D/O) and confirms POD.

### Phase 3 – File Closure (HOÀN THÀNH HỒ SƠ ĐƠN HÀNG)

1. Ops uploads final dossier (BL/AWB, CI, PL, customs docs, POD, etc.).
2. Accounting issues Debit Note / Final Debit Note.
3. KBI confirms debt/invoice.
4. File is closed after settlement.

## Trucking Quotation Principles

This section describes FDS's pricing and fuel-adjustment rules for trucking quotations to KBI.

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Quotation Round 1** | FDS quotes trucking at time of quotation, with fuel price on quotation date. Reference source: Petrolimex. |
| **Quotation Round 2** | Before/on delivery date, FDS updates price based on fuel price on delivery date if adjustment is needed. |
| **Fuel contribution ratio** | 36% of the selling price structure. |
| **Adjustment formula** | `New price = Initial price × [1 | fuel price change ratio × 36%]` |
| **Control point** | Sales must record: quotation date, fuel price on quotation date, delivery date, fuel price on delivery date for reconciliation. |

## Issue Resolution Process

### Step 1 – Identify the SOP step and responsible department

- Determine which SOP step the issue occurs at (or nearest step).
- Identify the responsible team: **Sales / Ops / Finance**.
- The first person receiving the issue notification is responsible for alerting the responsible department.
- The receiving department determines authority and specific resolver.

> **Note**: If the issue spans multiple steps, department Managers consult each other to identify the primary responsible department. If no department can be determined, the Director assigns responsibility; other departments cooperate.

### Step 2 – Determine issue level and management authority

Issues are classified into 3 levels by impact:

| Level | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Level 1** | Minor impact; can be resolved immediately upon discovery. Does not affect key shipment timelines or order costs. |
| **Level 2** | Significant impact; needs appropriate resolution time. May affect key shipment timelines or order costs if not resolved in time. |
| **Level 3** | Severe impact. Does not fall under Level 1 or Level 2. |

Authority matrix:

| Level | Executive (Direct work) | Manager | Director |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | **A** | I | |
| Level 2 | R | **A** | I |
| Level 3 | R | R | **A** |

Legend:
- **A** – Accountability: Primary responsible person; one per issue.
- **R** – Responsibility: Execute/coordinate under A's direction; one or more.
- **I** – Informed: Notified about the issue.

### Step 3 – Identify specific resolver

Based on Step 2 results, look up the personnel table to identify the specific resolver.
If no corresponding level personnel is available, the Director assigns a resolver per the role defined in Step 2.

## QA Acceptance

- Quotation is sent within 8 hours of request receipt.
- Draft BL/AWB is sent to KBI for review within SLA.
- KBI review of Draft BL/AWI is completed within 2 hours.
- Arrival Notice is sent at least 2 days before ATA.
- Customs draft declaration is submitted at least 3 days before ETA.
- Official declaration is submitted within 2 hours of KBI confirmation.
- Debit Note / Final Debit Note is issued within 3 hours of Final BL/AWB.
- Final dossier is uploaded within 5 days of ATA.
- All issues are classified by level (1/2/3) and assigned per authority matrix.
- FDS personnel directory is kept up to date.
